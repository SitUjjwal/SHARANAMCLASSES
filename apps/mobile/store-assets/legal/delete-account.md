## How to request deletion

1. Email **sharanam.sp@gmail.com**  
2. Subject: `Account deletion request`  
3. Include registered name, email/phone  
4. We verify identity and delete/anonymise data within **30 days**  
5. Confirmation by email  

## Data deleted

Profile, auth identifiers, enrollments, progress, test attempts, push tokens, support tickets (where feasible), profile uploads.

## Data that may be retained

- Payment/billing records: up to **7 years** (legal / tax / disputes)  
- Limited security logs: typically up to **90 days**  

## Partial data deletion (keep account)

Email subject `Data deletion request` listing what to remove.
