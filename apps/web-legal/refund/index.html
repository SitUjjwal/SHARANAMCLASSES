Refund Policy

Effective / Last updated: 4 August 2026
Contact: sharanam.sp@gmail.com

This Refund Policy applies to paid course enrollments and related digital learning purchases made through the SHARANAM CLASSES app or authorised channels.

1. Nature of products

SHARANAM CLASSES primarily sells digital educational services (course access, live class participation rights, notes/PDFs, tests). These are intangible goods delivered online.

2. General rule

All sales are final once course access has been granted, except where a refund is required by applicable Indian consumer law, this policy expressly allows a refund, or SHARANAM CLASSES approves a goodwill refund at its discretion.

3. Eligible refund situations (examples)

  • You were charged twice for the same enrollment due to a technical error
  • Payment succeeded but access was never unlocked within a reasonable time after verification
  • The course was cancelled by us and no suitable alternative was offered
  • A duplicate / wrong course was purchased and you notify us within 24 hours, and you have not substantially consumed content (see §5)

4. Non-refundable situations (typical)

  • You simply changed your mind after accessing lessons
  • You completed a significant portion of videos, notes, or tests
  • Live classes were missed due to your device, internet, or schedule
  • Exam results or learning outcomes did not meet personal expectations
  • Account sharing or Terms violations led to suspension

5. “Substantial use”

We consider substantial use if, for example, you have streamed multiple lessons, downloaded/opened multiple PDFs, or attempted graded tests under the enrollment.

6. How to request a refund

Email sharanam.sp@gmail.com with:

  • Registered name, phone/email
  • Order / payment ID (Razorpay or app receipt)
  • Course name
  • Reason and supporting screenshots

We aim to respond within 5–7 business days.

7. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible. Bank / UPI timelines may take 5–10 business days after we initiate the refund with the payment partner.

8. Partial refunds

Where fair, we may offer a partial refund or course credit instead of a full refund.

9. Promotional / bundled offers

Special promo prices, bundles, or scholarship seats may have different refund rules stated at purchase; those terms prevail for that offer.
