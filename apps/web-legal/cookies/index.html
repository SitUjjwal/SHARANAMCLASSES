This Cookie Policy explains how SHARANAM CLASSES uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. For personal data practices generally, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and SDK identifiers (on web).

2. Mobile app note

The SHARANAM CLASSES mobile app does not use browser cookies in the same way. It may use:

  • Secure storage for login sessions
  • Local preferences (language, notification toggles, theme)
  • Analytics/diagnostics SDKs if enabled in a release

App data practices are covered in the Privacy Policy and Data Safety disclosures.

3. Types of cookies we may use on the website

Type Purpose Examples
Strictly necessary Security, load balancing, basic site function Session cookies
Functional Remember language or UI choices Preference cookies
Analytics Understand traffic and page performance Privacy-friendly analytics (if enabled)
Marketing Only if we run ads/campaigns Third-party pixels (if enabled)

We aim to minimise cookies. Marketing cookies, if used, will be disclosed and controlled where required.

4. How to manage cookies

You can block or delete cookies via browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the website (e.g. logged-in admin or forms).

5. Third parties

If we embed YouTube, maps, payment pages, or social links, those third parties may set their own cookies under their policies.

6. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy; the “Last updated” date will change.
